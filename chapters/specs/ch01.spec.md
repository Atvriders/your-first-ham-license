## 3. Teaching beats (ordered `###` outline)

Condensed from `canon/research/r3-teaching-t1-t5.md` (T5) and `canon/research/r4-teaching-t6-t0.md` (T6) — read those for full per-question detail. Every beat ends by naming its pool group so the reader knows what it unlocks.

### Beat 1 — Electricity from zero: current, voltage, power (T5A)
- Water analogy: voltage = pressure, current = flow rate. Current is the flow of electrons, measured in amperes (T5A03, T5A01); **a difference in voltage causes electron flow** (T5A05 — quote the *revised* wording, see §8).
- Power = the rate at which electrical energy is used, measured in watts (T5A10, T5A02).
- Resistance opposes every kind of current flow — DC, AC, and RF (T5A11).
- Conductors vs insulators: metals conduct because they have many free electrons (T5A07); glass is a good insulator (T5A08).
- Vocabulary: current, voltage, power, resistance, conductor, insulator, ampere, volt, watt, ohm.

### Beat 2 — AC, DC, frequency, and wavelength (rest of T5A)
- Alternating current alternates between positive and negative directions (T5A09; "positive and zero" is pulsating DC, a distractor).
- Frequency = complete cycles per second; unit hertz (T5A04, T5A06).
- Gentle bridge to radio: a radio wave has a frequency and a wavelength, related by the speed of light (f·λ = c; full drill is ch03's job — here just name the relationship). Figure: `{{fig:ch01-ac-dc-waveforms}}`, then `{{fig:ch01-wavelength-freq}}`.
- Vocabulary: alternating current (AC), direct current (DC), frequency, hertz, wavelength.

### Beat 3 — Units, prefixes, and decibels gently (T5B)
- The metric prefix ladder in three-order-of-magnitude steps: pico (10⁻¹²) → nano (10⁻⁹) → micro (10⁻⁶) → milli (10⁻³) → base → kilo (10³) → mega (10⁶) → giga (10⁹). Toward a smaller unit, multiply; toward a larger, divide. Figure: `{{fig:ch01-prefix-ladder}}`.
- Drill with the pool's own numbers (see §5).
- Decibels by anchors, **not** logarithms: ×2 ≈ 3 dB, ×4 ≈ 6 dB (so ÷4 ≈ −6 dB), ×10 = 10 dB; positive = increase, negative = decrease. The defining formula dB = 10·log₁₀(P₂/P₁) may appear only in a `> **The math, if you want it:**` sidebar (canon §3).
- Vocabulary: milli-, micro-, nano-, pico-, kilo-, mega-, giga-, decibel (dB).

### Beat 4 — Capacitance, inductance, impedance, and case-sensitive units (T5C first half)
- Capacitance stores energy in an **electric** field; unit farad (T5C01, T5C02). Inductance stores energy in a **magnetic** field; unit henry (T5C03, T5C04). Teach the pairing to stop the swap.
- Impedance = opposition to AC current flow (resistance plus reactance); unit ohm (T5C12, T5C05).
- Unit abbreviations are case-sensitive and genuinely tested: kHz (lowercase k), MHz (capital M), capital H in both (T5C06, T5C07).
- Vocabulary: capacitance, farad, inductance, henry, impedance, reactance, kHz, MHz.

### Beat 5 — The power law: P = E × I (T5C second half)
- P = E × I, rearranged I = P/E and E = P/I (T5C08). Drill with T5C09–T5C11 numbers (see §5).
- **Notation law (canon §3):** prose in this book uses V and × (V = I × R, P = V × I); the pool prints E and "x". Teach the equivalence explicitly on first use ("E and V both mean volts"), and keep the pool's E/x form only inside verbatim quotes.
- Vocabulary: (none new — apply Beat 1 terms).

### Beat 6 — Ohm's law and series/parallel circuits (T5D)
- E = I × R, I = E / R, R = E / I (T5D01–T5D03); teach the cover-the-unknown triangle. Figures: `{{fig:ch01-ohms-law-triangle}}`.
- Drill the triangle in all three orientations with the pool's own numbers (see §5); every distractor is the wrong operation on the same two numbers.
- Series circuit: same current through all components (T5D13); parallel circuit: same voltage across all (T5D14). Memory hook: "series = same current." Figure: `{{fig:ch01-series-parallel}}`.
- Vocabulary: Ohm's law, series circuit, parallel circuit.

### Beat 7 — Passive components, switches, batteries (T6A)
- Resistor opposes (limits) current (T6A01); potentiometer = adjustable resistor controlling resistance (T6A02, T6A03).
- Capacitor = two conductive plates separated by a dielectric, stores energy in an electric field (T6A04, T6A05); inductor = a coil of wire, stores energy in a magnetic field (T6A06, T6A07).
- Switch nomenclature: pole = circuits handled, throw = contact positions per pole; SPDT connects one circuit to either of two others (T6A08); component 3 in pool Figure T-2 is SPST (T6A09).
- Battery chemistries: nickel-metal hydride, lithium-ion, lead-acid, nickel-cadmium are rechargeable (T6A10); **carbon-zinc is the not-rechargeable one** (T6A11).
- Vocabulary: resistor, potentiometer, capacitor, dielectric, inductor, pole, throw, SPST, SPDT, rechargeable, carbon-zinc, NiMH, lithium-ion, lead-acid.

### Beat 8 — Semiconductors (T6B)
- Diode: one-way current (T6B02); anode/cathode (T6B09); cathode stripe on the physical part (T6B06); forward voltage drop differs among diode types (T6B01).
- LED = diode that emits light when forward current flows (T6B07).
- Transistor: three regions of semiconductor (T6B04); electronic switch (T6B03) or amplifier providing power gain (T6B10); gain = output compared to input — voltage, current, or power (T6B11).
- Two families, two electrode sets: BJT = emitter/base/collector (T6B12); FET = gate/drain/source (T6B05); FET = Field Effect Transistor (T6B08). Drill the three electrode sets (diode/BJT/FET) as a table.
- Vocabulary: semiconductor, diode, anode, cathode, forward voltage drop, LED, forward current, transistor, BJT, FET, emitter/base/collector, gate/drain/source, gain.

### Beat 9 — Reading schematics and the three pool figures (T6C + T6A09 + T6D10)
- A schematic is drawn with standard component symbols and shows **how components connect** — not wire lengths or physical appearance (T6C01, T6C12). Symbols, not pictures.
- Walk the symbol panel figure, then each pool figure redraw with its circuit story (canon §1.4 is the binding description — positions/labels exact): Figure T-1 transistor lamp-switch (1 resistor, 2 NPN transistor, 3 lamp, 4 battery, 5 ground); Figure T-2 AC power supply (chain: source → fuse → SPST switch → transformer → rectifier diode → filter capacitor → resistor+LED indicator → variable resistor → Zener); Figure T-3 antenna tuner T-network (1 connector, 2 variable capacitor, 3 variable inductor, 4 antenna).
- Warn about the traps: in T-3 the asked component 3 is the variable **inductor** while adjacent component 2 is the variable **capacitor** (T6C10's trap); teach position numbers, not just symbols. In T-1, the transistor's function is "control the flow of current" (T6D10).
- Figures: `{{fig:ch01-component-symbols}}`, `{{fig:ch01-pool-fig-t1}}`, `{{fig:ch01-pool-fig-t2}}`, `{{fig:ch01-pool-fig-t3}}`.
- Vocabulary: schematic, component symbol, ground symbol, node/junction.

### Beat 10 — What circuits do (T6D)
- Power-supply chain: transformer → rectifier → filter → regulator. Rectifier: AC into varying (pulsating) DC (T6D01); transformer: AC voltage up/down, never to DC (T6D06); regulator: holds output voltage steady (T6D05).
- Relay = electrically controlled switch (T6D02); shielded wire keeps unwanted signals from coupling in or out (T6D03); meter displays an electrical quantity (T6D04); LEDs are the standard visual indicator (T6D07).
- Inductor + capacitor = resonant (tuned) circuit — the frequency-selecting circuit (T6D08, T6D11); IC = many semiconductors in one package (T6D09).
- Vocabulary: rectifier, regulator, transformer, relay, shielded wire, meter, indicator, resonant/tuned circuit, integrated circuit (IC), Zener diode.

## 5. Worked examples (use the pool's own numbers — at least one `> **Worked example:**` blockquote; two or three short ones are welcome here)

- Ohm's law (T5D05): "A circuit has 12 V across a resistor and 1.5 A through it — what's the resistance? R = E / I = 12 ÷ 1.5 = **8 Ω**." (Further drill numbers available: T5D04 90 V ÷ 3 A = 30 Ω; T5D09 240 V ÷ 24 Ω = 10 A; T5D12 2 A × 10 Ω = 20 V.)
- Power law (T5C09): "Your radio draws 10 A from a 13.8 V supply — how much power? P = E × I = 13.8 × 10 = **138 W**." (Also: T5C10 12 V × 2.5 A = 30 W; T5C11 120 W ÷ 12 V = 10 A.)
- Prefix conversion (T5B07): "3.525 MHz in kHz? Toward the smaller unit, multiply: 3.525 × 1000 = **3525 kHz**." (Also: T5B01 1.5 A = 1500 mA; T5B13 2425 MHz = 2.425 GHz.)
- Decibels (T5B10): "Power drops from 12 W to 3 W — how many dB? 3 W is one quarter of 12 W, and one quarter ≈ **−6 dB**."
