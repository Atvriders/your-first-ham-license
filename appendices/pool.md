## Appendix A: The Complete 2026–2030 Technician Question Pool

This appendix is the complete 2026–2030 Technician question pool: all 409 questions, exactly as the NCVEC Question Pool Committee published them, each with its four choices and the keyed answer. The NCVEC released this pool into the public domain on December 18, 2025, so it is reproduced here verbatim, wording and punctuation included. It is valid for every Technician exam given from 2026-07-01 through 2030-06-30, and it already incorporates the revision issued 2026-02-19, which updated four questions (T1C01, T5A05, T7A09, and T0A10).

How to use it: every group of questions maps to one chapter of this book — read that chapter first, then come back and drill its questions here. Each entry shows the question and choices verbatim, then the correct answer with a one-line plain-language why naming the chapter that teaches it. Beneath each entry, the Published ID line carries the question's official answer letter and Part 97 rule reference exactly as printed in the pool document — including two small printing quirks in the original, preserved rather than repaired.

The exam itself draws 35 questions from this pool — one from each of the 35 groups — and 26 correct answers pass. Three of the questions refer to official figures: Figure T-1 (a transistor switch circuit), Figure T-2 (an AC power supply), and Figure T-3 (an antenna tuner T-network). All three are redrawn here as original diagrams, embedded with the questions that use them in subelement T6. When you are ready to test yourself, the companion practice test at practice.html draws a valid 35-question practice exam — one question per group — from the same canonical pool.
